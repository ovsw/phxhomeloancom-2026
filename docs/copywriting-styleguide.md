## Content fundamentals

**Voice:** first-person-plural "we/our team" mixed with direct second-person
"you/your" — never distant third person. Warm, plain-spoken, a little folksy,
never salesy. Explicitly anti-jargon: the blog section is literally called
*"Plain-English takes on the market."*

**Sentence style:** short declaratives, contractions used freely ("don't",
"you're", "it's"), occasional rhetorical question ("Wondering if now is the
right time to lock a rate?"). Blog posts are written in first person, casual
register — "So I found this kind of interesting" — as if Jimmy is
talking directly to the reader, not a press release.

**Trust-first framing:** nearly every section anchors a claim in a verifiable
fact — "Top 1% Mortgage Loan Originators… 2019, according to Mortgage
Executive Magazine," "4.9/5 · 100 Reviews," "NMLS# 184169." Military service
is a recurring, sincere credibility marker (USMC veteran, deployed to Iraq),
never played for cheap patriotism — it's tied back to a value ("how you make
people feel matters more than how much you know").

**Casing:** sentence case for headlines and body; eyebrow labels are
ALL-CAPS with wide letter-spacing ("TYPES OF LOANS", "MEET JIMMY VERCELLINO").
No emoji in UI copy; the one blog post uses 📈/📉 as inline list-heading
markers, which is the *only* place emoji appear.

**Person:** "you/your" dominates in persuasive/marketing copy (hero, CTAs);
switches to "he/his/Jimmy" in third person for bio and team copy.

**Compliance voice:** footer legal copy is formal, precise, and unstyled —
"This is not an offer to enter into an agreement… Equal Housing Lender" — a
deliberate tonal break from the warm marketing copy elsewhere, and it must
never be rewritten casually.

**No hype:** copy avoids superlatives without receipts. Even confident claims
("Personalized service for your home loan in Phoenix") are grounded by a
concrete next step (Schedule a Consultation / Explore Loan Options), not
adjectives.
